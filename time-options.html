Four ways to show that a mask slows the clock

Drafts for the Time adds up page. Each one shows the same idea: the longer you breathe the same air, the more you take in, and a mask's protection factor slows that down. Pick your mask in each one and compare.

Option 1

The race

Two faces breathe the same air from 7 am to 11 pm, one with no mask and one with yours. A jar under each fills up as the day goes on.

Open the race → Option 2

The heap

Particles rain down on every mask at once. The mask catches most of them; what gets through piles up underneath, all day.

Open the heap → Option 3

The crowded room

A working day in an office with one ill person. Move yourself around, open the windows and change masks to see what each person breathes in.

Open the room → Option 4

Scroll through a day

Scroll from breakfast to bed through a train, an office, a café and a pub, and watch the time in shared air add up.
